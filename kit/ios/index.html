      Care notes

      Water when the top few centimetres of soil are dry; most plants here would rather be a little thirsty than sit in a wet pot.

      Turn the pot a quarter turn each week so the plant grows evenly instead of leaning towards the window.

      Wipe broad leaves with a damp cloth every month or so; dust blocks the light they need.

      Feed in spring and summer only, at half the strength the bottle suggests.

      Yellow lower leaves usually mean too much water; brown crispy tips usually mean dry air.

      Repot when roots come out of the drainage holes, into a pot one size bigger, not more.
